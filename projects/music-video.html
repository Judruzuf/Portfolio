Music Video

A music video created for an original song. This project showcases my creative direction and video production skills.

Project Details

This project was a made from the ground up by a team of four people for university. We did everything ourselves, from sitting in the class brainstorming to studio sessions where the song was recorded to capturing it on camera and editing the video. For the video, we went for showing how the track was actually made to showing what effect it has when played at a party.
